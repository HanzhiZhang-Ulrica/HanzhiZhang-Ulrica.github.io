## 🎓 Academic Pursuits

Hanzhi Zhang is currently a **PhD Candidate in Computer Science**, directed by [Dr. Yunhe Feng](https://yunhefeng.me/) in the [Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the University of North Texas (UNT), Denton, TX](https://computerscience.engineering.unt.edu/), with expected graduation in **May 2027**. She is a member of the [**Responsible AI Lab**](https://yunhefeng.me/lab/) and the [**LLaVi Lab**](https://llavi-lab.github.io/) at UNT. Her academic journey is driven by a profound interest in exploring the efficiency and reliability of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Her research primarily focuses on **⚡ Efficient AI Systems**, delving into **hardware-aware LLM inference**, **sparse and mixed-precision computation**, **post-training quantization**, and **GPU optimization and HPC**. She also explores **🤖 Reliable and Responsible AI**, including **🌍 multilingual fact verification**, **🧠 AI Hallucination**, and **👁️ safety-critical perception**. Her work aims to forge a path towards more accountable, transparent, and efficient AI systems.

## 📚 Professional Background

Hanzhi completed her **📊 MS in Data Science** with **🏆 Distinction** from the [University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/) (2021-2022) and her **💻 BS in Computer Science and Technology (Honours)** from [Xiamen University](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/) (2017-2021). She previously worked as a **💾 Storage Backend Intern** at **OPPO, Inc.** (2020-2021), focusing on file systems development.
