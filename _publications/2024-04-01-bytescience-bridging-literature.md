ByteScience introduces an innovative framework that bridges the gap between unstructured scientific literature and structured data by leveraging auto fine-tuned large language models operating at token granularity. This approach enables enhanced knowledge extraction and processing, facilitating better integration of textual scientific information with structured databases and improving automated scientific data analysis.
