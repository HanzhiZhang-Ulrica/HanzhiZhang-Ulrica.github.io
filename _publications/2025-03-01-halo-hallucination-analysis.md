HALO introduces a novel framework designed to enhance the accuracy and reliability of medical question-answering (QA) systems by focusing on the detection and mitigation of hallucinations. The approach generates multiple variations of queries using LLMs and retrieves relevant information from external knowledge bases to enrich context. The framework achieved notable improvements in accuracy for both open-source and commercial LLMs, such as Llama-3.1 (from 44% to 65%) and ChatGPT (from 56% to 70%). Available at: https://github.com/ResponsibleAILab/HALO
