This paper introduces D2SO, a specialized framework for detecting distant and small objects in vision-based autonomous vehicle systems, enhancing safety and situational awareness. The work addresses the critical challenge of identifying distant objects, each smaller than 24×24 pixels, which is essential for trustworthy autonomous systems. **Note: This paper will be presented at IEEE MOST 2025 (May 4-6, 2025) but is not yet published.**
